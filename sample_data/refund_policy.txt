Acme Cloud Storage - Refund Policy

Refunds for annual subscriptions are issued within five business days of a
verified cancellation request. Monthly subscriptions are not eligible for
partial-period refunds; access continues until the end of the current
billing cycle. Store credit, where offered as an alternative, is applied
immediately at checkout and does not require the five-day processing
window. Enterprise contracts follow the refund terms negotiated in the
master service agreement rather than this default policy.
